## 🇵🇰 **Pakistan-Specific Custom Apps**

### 1. **TaxCompliancePakistan** 📊
Complete FBR (Federal Board of Revenue) compliance automation

**Features:**
- ✅ **Sales Tax**: Automatic FBR sales tax rate calculation
- ✅ **Withholding Tax (WHT)**: Sections-based WHT on purchases/sales
- ✅ **WHT Exemptions**: Manage tax exemptions by supplier/customer
- ✅ **Annex A Report**: Automated monthly sales tax annexure
- ✅ **Annex C Report**: Purchase-side tax documentation
- ✅ **Tax Categories**: Industry-specific tax rates pre-configured

**Business Value:**  
*No manual tax calculations. No FBR filing errors. Audit-ready reports at one click.*

---

### 2. **ImportManager** 🚢
End-to-end import workflow and costing automation

**Features:**
- ✅ **Letter of Credit (LC)**: Create, track, and settle LCs
- ✅ **Import Documents**: GD (Goods Declaration), IGM tracking
- ✅ **Customs Duties**: Calculate customs, sales tax, income tax, regulatory duties
- ✅ **Charge Allocation**: Distribute freight/insurance/clearing costs across items
- ✅ **LC Outstanding Report**: Track open LCs and pending settlements
- ✅ **Landed Cost**: Automatic calculation of true import cost per item
- ✅ **Workflow**: Track document flow from opening to settlement

**Business Value:**  
*Know your true import costs. Track every rupee from LC opening to warehouse. No surprise costs.*

---

### 3. **SpotLedger HR** 👥
Advanced factory attendance with Pakistani labor practices

**Features:**
- ✅ **Grace Periods**: Configurable check-in/check-out grace (typically 15 min)
- ✅ **Friday Logic**: Separate timings + extended prayer break (60 min)
- ✅ **Overtime Calculation**: Auto-calculate OT hours beyond required shift
- ✅ **Deficiency Tracking**: Track shortfall when employees leave early/arrive late
- ✅ **Gazetted Holidays**: Auto-detect Eid, Independence Day, etc. with OT multipliers
- ✅ **Bulk Attendance**: Edit 1,000+ attendance records in one screen
- ✅ **Biometric Integration**: Auto-fetch first IN and last OUT of day
- ✅ **Overnight Shifts**: Handle night shifts crossing midnight

**Business Value:**  
*HR saves 20+ hours/month. Zero disputes. Employees see transparent calculations. Payroll ready data.*

---

### 4. **Manufacturing Costing** 🏭
Advanced product costing and expense allocation for complex manufacturing

**Features:**
- ✅ **Product Costing Sheets**: Detailed cost breakdowns per product
- ✅ **Material Specifications**: Track material types, sizes, and grades
- ✅ **Expense Allocation**: Distribute overhead costs across production
- ✅ **Work Order Integration**: Link costing to production orders
- ✅ **Quality Inspection**: Serial number tracking in QC
- ✅ **Production Order Reports**: Urdu-enabled print formats for shop floor
- ✅ **Rework Tracking**: Track rework entries and serial numbers

**Business Value:**  
*Know exact cost per product. Price accurately. Track quality by serial number. Urdu reports for shop floor.*
